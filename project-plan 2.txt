1.ORGANISATION OVERVIEW
Logan`s Coffee Co is a local coffee shop located in Pretoria CBD , established in 2024.
HISTORY: The shop started to provide quality coffee and comfortable workspace for students and professionals in the city,
MISSION: "To fuel the city  with great coffee and good vibes"
VISSION: "To become Pretoria`s top spot for coffee, connection and community."
TARGET AUDIENCE: Students and workers aged 18-35 mostly based around Pretoria who need affordable coffee, Wi-Fi, and a place to study or have meetings.

2.WEBSITE GOALS AND OBJECTIVES
Goal 1: Increase foot traffic to the physical store by 30%
Goal 2: Provide an easy way for customers to view the menu and prices online
Goal 3: Allow customers to connect the shop for enquires and bookings
KPIS:
-400 websites visits per month
-25 contact/enquiry form submissions per month

3.CURRENT WEBSITE ANALYSIS
The organisation currently does not have a website .This is a weakness because potential customers cannot find information about the menu, location or opening hours online. The opportunity is to build a new responsive website from scratch to establish an online presence.

4.PROPOSED WEBSITE FEATURES AND FUNTIONALITY
The website will have 5 main pages: Home, About, Services/Menu, Gallery, Contact, and Enquiry.
Key Features:
-Responsive design for mobile and desktop
-Online menu with prices and images
-Contact form for enquires
-Google Maps integration for location
-Images gallery of products
-Fast loading and SEO optimizes

5.DESIGN AND USER EXPERIENCE
Colour Scheme: Brown, Cream, Dark Brown to reflect coffee and warmth 
Typography: Clear, readable fonts. Heading bold, body text simple
Layout: Clean, mobile-first design with large images and clear navigation
UX: Easy to find menu and contact info.
Fast loading. All images will have alt text.
