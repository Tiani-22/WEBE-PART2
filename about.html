Logan's Coffee Co.

Our Story

Founded in 2024, Logan's Coffee Co. started with one goal: to create a space where Pretoria can come together over great coffee, welcoming space for students and workers in Pretoria CBD. Our passion for coffee and community drives everything we do, from sourcing the finest beans to crafting the perfect cup.

We believe in quality, sustainability, and the power of a good cup of coffee to bring people together. Our team is dedicated to providing an exceptional experience for every customer, whether you're here for a quick espresso or a leisurely afternoon with friends.

Our Values

  • Quality: We are committed to serving the highest quality coffee and pastries.
  • Community: We strive to create a welcoming environment for everyone.
  • Sustainability: We source our beans responsibly and aim to minimize our environmental impact.
  • Innovation: We continuously explore new flavors and brewing techniques to delight our customers.

Our Mission

To serve quality coffee and create a welcoming environment for our community.

To fuel the city with great coffee, good vibes, and exceptional service.
